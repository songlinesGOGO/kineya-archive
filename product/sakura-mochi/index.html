Description

Wagashi are often enjoyed with tea, particularly matcha (powdered green tea). The sweetness of the wagashi helps to balance the bitter taste of the tea and create a harmonious taste experience. If you’re new to matcha, start with a small amount and gradually increase the amount as you become more accustomed to the taste.
